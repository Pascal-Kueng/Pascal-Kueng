# Pascal Küng

Postdoctoral researcher in Applied Social and Health Psychology at the University of Zurich. I study social support and social control in close relationships, with a focus on physical activity, sedentary behaviour, ecological momentary assessment, and dyadic intensive longitudinal methods.

I also develop open-source research software and freely available teaching materials:

- [dyadMLM](https://pascal-kueng.github.io/dyadMLM/) — tools for cross-sectional and intensive longitudinal dyadic multilevel models ([concept DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22047083))
- [wbCorr](https://pascal-kueng.github.io/wbCorr/) — within- and between-cluster correlations ([concept DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20357592))
- [Bayesian APIM and DIM tutorial](https://pascal-kueng.github.io/05DyadicDataAnalysis/) — brms-based materials for distinguishable and exchangeable dyads ([concept DOI](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17400655))
